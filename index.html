Before you start

An introduction to the course

Congratulations on taking this step. You're about to learn Head Spa, a scalp care experience that is growing fast around the world and can become a very profitable service for our salon.

The method was designed for professionals who want to learn in a simple, clear way, without unnecessary complications. There's no theory here that you can't put into practice.

You'll follow a complete method, step by step: from mastering the technique and organising the service, to setting prices and the best way to offer it to our clients.

  • 1
    Follow the modules in orderStart with the basics and move forward module by module. Each part builds on what you learned before.
  • 2
    Use the reference videosSome modules include reference videos to help you see and better understand the techniques.
  • 3
    Make the most of the bonusesThey help you put it into practice faster, from organising the new service to attracting your first clients.
  • 4
    Enjoy the processThe goal is simple: a method that actually works in practice. Relax, take this time for yourself and let's get started.
